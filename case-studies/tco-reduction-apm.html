ISQM TCO Reduction Support Assistant Tree Troubleshooting Guide

Reducing Support Costs Through
Data-Driven Documentation

An ISQM-driven analysis of closed support cases in SAP Asset Performance Management to identify recurring ticket patterns, map documentation gaps, and build content that deflects tickets before customers need to raise them.

2,753 Closed cases analyzed (2023–2026)
94.66% Resolved with zero doc coverage
17 Ticket patterns identified
Ongoing Active workstream

Context

The problem, the tool, and the scope.

The problem

SAP APM generates a steady volume of customer support tickets. Analysis revealed that 94.66% of closed cases (across 2,753 tickets over four years) were resolved with zero contribution from any documentation, KBA, or FAQ. Every ticket was handled entirely by the engineering or support team. That's a direct cost with no content safety net.

The goal was to understand why tickets were arriving and what content could have deflected them before a case was opened. A deflection rate of even 10–15% would mean 275–400 fewer tickets per cycle handled by support.

The tool: ISQM

ISQM (Intelligent Support Queue Management) analyzes closed support tickets and clusters them into patterns and subpatterns. It surfaces resolution categories, team distribution, and solution reuse rates, giving a structured view of where documentation investment would have the highest return.

Approach

Turning ticket data into actionable documentation content.

1

Extract and rank patterns

Used ISQM to identify general patterns and subpatterns across the APM ticket history. Ranked by volume to prioritize the highest-impact areas first.

2

Map to the support assistant tree

Mapped each ISQM pattern to the existing SAP APM support assistant tree. Identified nodes with no content recommendations or functional area questions, and prioritized them for development based on ticket volume.

3

Draft tree node content

For each gap node, defined the functional area questions a customer sees during ticket creation, and the content recommendations (SAP Help Portal links, KBAs, and guides) surfaced at that node in me.sap.com.

4

Write troubleshooting guide entries

Created structured troubleshooting entries (Issue / Possible Causes / Resolution) for the top recurring subpatterns, starting with the highest-volume areas and expanding iteratively each week.

Output

What was delivered.

Support assistant tree nodes

New and expanded tree nodes covering the identified ticket patterns, each with:

  • Functional area questions to categorize the issue at case creation
  • Content recommendations linking to SAP Help Portal topics, KBAs, and operational guides
  • Coverage prioritized by ticket volume

These nodes feed directly into me.sap.com, surfacing relevant help before a case is submitted.

Troubleshooting guide entries

Structured entries added to the SAP APM Troubleshooting Guide on SAP Help Portal, covering the most frequently recurring issues. Each entry follows the Issue / Possible Causes / Resolution format, scoped by component.

New entries are added iteratively as additional patterns are reviewed and validated.

Weekly Progress

An ongoing log of areas covered, updated as the workstream progresses.

W2

SAP Notes analysis and tree content expansion

Processed the top SAP Notes from the most-used solutions list. For each note, mapped it to the correct support assistant tree node, proposed new questions and content recommendations, and drafted troubleshooting guide entries. Work this week introduced a new tree node for analytics dashboard issues and established a placement rule distinguishing configuration-side questions (Communication Systems node) from sync error routing (Integration and Sync Errors node). Also updated the internal ISQM wiki with the latest data and corrected node placements from the previous week's ticket analysis.

W1

Kickoff: ISQM analysis and tree gap mapping

Completed initial ISQM analysis. Identified 17 general ticket patterns and mapped each to the existing support assistant tree. Prioritized nodes by volume. Drafted initial tree content for the Integration and Connectivity cluster (highest-volume area) and the Licensing & Subscription node.

Why It Matters

The broader value of this workstream.

Deflection, not just documentation

Most documentation work focuses on coverage. This workstream focuses on interception, meeting customers at the exact moment they are about to open a ticket, with the right content to resolve it themselves. The support assistant tree is that intervention point.

Repeatable method

The ISQM → gap mapping → tree content workflow is repeatable. Each analysis cycle can be used to identify new emerging patterns, validate whether previously addressed areas have improved, and queue the next round of content work, making TCO reduction a continuous process rather than a one-time effort.
